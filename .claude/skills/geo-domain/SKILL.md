# GEO / AI Search Visibility — Domain Reference

## The shift, in the numbers that matter

- ChatGPT reached ~900M weekly active users by Feb 2026, up from ~400M a year earlier.
- ~68% of US Google searches end without a click; ~83% when an AI Overview appears; ~93% for AI Mode sessions.
- Organic CTR falls ~61% when an AI Overview is present — but brands **cited inside it** earn ~35% more organic clicks.
- **Only ~12–14% of URLs cited by AI engines rank in Google's top ten for the same query.**
- ~82% of AI citations come from **earned media**, not owned or paid.
- AI referral traffic converts ~31% better than non-AI traffic.

The single most important line for positioning: existing SEO investment does not
automatically buy AI visibility, and existing SEO tooling cannot measure it.

## The gap we attack

Agency executives report that the same prompts through three tools give three
different answers. An Aug 2026 audit of 72 AI-visibility tools found 34 assert
precision with no evidence and only 6 publish a checkable methodology.

There is no shared definition of AI share of voice, no public benchmark prompt
set, and no convention on how many runs get averaged. That vacuum is the opening.

## Copy rules

- Never claim precision we cannot evidence. The brand is built on the opposite.
- Never say "we're more accurate". Say "our number comes with its interval and its method".
- Avoid "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlock". The audience is sceptical practitioners.
- Lead with the disclosure gap, not with features.
