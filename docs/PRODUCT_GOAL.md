# BlipRank — Product Goal

> **This is the one canonical reference every session checks its work against.**
> It is written verbatim from the owner's statement of 2026-09-09. Do not
> paraphrase it, re-explain it, or restate it differently elsewhere. Audits,
> plans and reviews are structured against these eleven points, by number.

**The complete BlipRank goal:**

1. **Core product**: measures whether a business gets mentioned by AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews) in response to real buyer questions. Reports an honest mention rate with a genuine Wilson confidence interval — never a fake precise number. This is a real, sellable product for real clients, not a demo.
2. **Two personas, genuinely separate experiences**: a brand owner checking their own visibility, and an agency managing multiple clients — served from shared architecture but never merged into one confusing interface.
3. **Any real domain must work**: real content-based classification (not just matching the domain string), with the taxonomy able to grow itself for categories that don't exist yet — and competitors are never invented, only ever surfaced from what AI engines actually say.
4. **Dual-audience usability from the same data**: a simple/plain-language view for non-technical users, an expandable detailed view for technical/agency users — not two different products.
5. **Full transparency, properly visualized**: every prompt and its real per-engine answer must be inspectable in raw form (the evidence of last resort) — but the primary experience needs genuinely purpose-built visualizations per kind of insight, not raw tables or logs.
6. **Real recurring operation**: pricing already promises "prompts re-check daily" — this must actually run as real automated daily collection with real trends, not a one-time report.
7. **User correctability without breaking integrity**: a wrong category, an unfair competitor set, custom prompts — all correctable, all versioned, never silently re-derived.
8. **Outsmart RankAI within an honest, bounded scope**: measurement, reporting, ranking, and diagnostics — explicitly never content generation or auto-publishing. That boundary is deliberate.
9. **Absolute integrity of every number**: no invented data, no confidently-wrong scores, real spend control everywhere, and scoring versions that never silently change what a historical number means.
10. **Production-grade engineering**: no cheap or temporary fixes, root-caused weak threads, architecture any developer could understand, genuinely hardened against attack, real automated test coverage, hostile review before anything is called done.
11. **Rich, complete delivery over rushed delivery**: quality and thoroughness take priority over speed.
